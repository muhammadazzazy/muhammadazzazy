I am a software engineer and technologist with about 2 years of experience in the domains of software development, automation, research, and information technology (IT) operations. Currently, I am a Full-Stack Engineer at [Deloitte](https://deloitte.com) Innovation Hub (DIH), where I have gained hands-on experience in Python, Spring Boot, React, Docker, Postgres, Google Cloud Platform (GCP), and Azure Logic Apps.

Previously, I worked at [Huawei](https://huawei.com) as an Operations Engineer, where I managed and monitored several Linux nodes, which enhanced my Linux system administration and troubleshooting skills. At Huawei, I gained application support experience, supporting operations for [Telkom](https://telkom.co.za) which is the largest telecom operator in South Africa. In June 2025, I earned the [Shubhra Kar Linux Foundation Training (LiFT) Scholarship](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/about/lift-scholarships), which enabled me to earn the LFCA: Linux Foundation Certified IT Associate certification. I was a Research Assistant at The American University in Cairo (AUC), where I developed [FebriLogic](https://febrilogic.com), a web application that helps clinical practitioners diagnose acute febrile illnesses (AFIs). After graduation, I was a Full-Stack Engineering Intern at [PricewaterhouseCoopers (PwC)](https://pwc.com) Egypt Technology & Innovation Center (ETIC), where I gained hands-on experience building backend systems in Python (Quart) during a couple of engagements with PwC UK. I also supported the development of the frontend components using Next.js and the business logic using Node.js for the Budget Module, which is an internal tool for rate card calculations.
