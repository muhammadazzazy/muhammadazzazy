I am a Full-Stack Engineer at [Deloitte](https://deloitte.com) Innovation Hub (DIH) with a background in software engineering, research, and telecom operations.

In January 2025, I graduated in Computer Engineering from [The American University in Cairo (AUC)](https://aucegypt.edu). I was a Full-Stack Engineering Intern at [PricewaterhouseCoopers (PwC)](https://pwc.com) Egypt Technology & Innovation Center (ETIC) where I contributed to projects recognized by engineers at PwC UK, including work featured in a [Medium article](https://medium.com/@bharathnatarajan101/genai-contract-processing-real-world-challenges-1e4396d78c20) by manager [Bharath Natarajan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/bharathnatarajanuk/). I have developed [FebriLogic](https://www.febrilogic.com), a tool that assists healthcare professionals in diagnosing patients with acute febrile illnesses (AFIs). Previously, I have worked at [Huawei](https://huawei.com) supporting Value-Added Services (VAS) applications for [Telkom South Africa](https://telkom.co.za).
